I am a Lecturer in the Faculty of Electrical Engineering at Thuyloi University, Hanoi, Vietnam, teaching in the Control Engineering and Automation and Robot Engineering and Intelligent Control programs. I earned my M.Sc. (with Distinction, GPA 3.72/4.00) and B.Sc. (5-year program) in Control Engineering and Automation from Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST). Before joining Thuyloi University, I spent four years at HUST as a Research Assistant and Teaching Assistant, working under the Motion Control and Applied Robotics Laboratory on research projects spanning multi-shaft drive systems, electric vehicles, and non-contact drive systems.

My research focuses on nonlinear and fault-tolerant control for electromechanical systems, including electric drives, electric vehicles, and renewable energy applications, with an emphasis on prescribed-/fixed-time convergence, robustness to input saturation and time delays, and validation through MIL/PIL/HIL simulation. I have published 15+ peer-reviewed journal papers, including in ISA Transactions, IEEE Access, and the International Journal of Robust and Nonlinear Control, and am a co-inventor on a pending Vietnamese patent on conical active magnetic bearings. I currently serve as scientific secretary and research team member on funded projects supported by the Vingroup Innovation Foundation (VINIF) and NAFOSTED.

I am currently seeking PhD opportunities in nonlinear and fault-tolerant control for electromechanical systems and robotics. If you are looking for a prospective PhD student or would like to discuss potential supervision, please feel free to reach out at [thinhld@tlu.edu.vn](mailto:thinhld@tlu.edu.vn).
