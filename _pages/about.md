I am a Lecturer in the Faculty of Electrical Engineering at Thuyloi University, Vietnam, and a graduate of Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST), where I earned my M.Sc. (with Distinction, GPA 3.72/4.00) and B.Eng. in Control Engineering and Automation.

My research focuses on nonlinear and fault-tolerant control for electromechanical systems, including electric drives, electric vehicles, and renewable energy applications — with an emphasis on prescribed-/fixed-time convergence, robustness to input saturation and time delays, and validation through MIL/PIL/HIL simulation. I have published 15+ peer-reviewed journal papers (ISA Transactions, IEEE Access, International Journal of Robust and Nonlinear Control, among others) and am a co-inventor on a pending Vietnamese patent on conical active magnetic bearings.

Feel free to reach out at [thinhld@tlu.edu.vn](mailto:thinhld@tlu.edu.vn).
