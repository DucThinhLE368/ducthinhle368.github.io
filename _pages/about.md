I am a Lecturer in the Department of Robot Engineering and Intelligent Systems at Thuyloi University, Hanoi, Vietnam. I earned my M.Sc. (with Distinction, GPA 3.72/4.00) and B.Sc. (5-year program) in Control Engineering and Automation from Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST).

My research focuses on electromechanical systems and robotics, multi-agent systems, transportation vehicles, energy management, and power electronics. I have published <a href="/publications/"><strong class="cv-highlight">15+ peer-reviewed journal papers</strong></a> and am a <a href="/patents/"><strong class="cv-highlight">co-inventor on a pending Vietnamese patent</strong></a> on conical active magnetic bearings. I currently serve as scientific secretary and research team member on <a href="/projects/"><strong class="cv-highlight">funded projects supported by VINIF and NAFOSTED</strong></a>.

**I am currently seeking PhD opportunities** in these areas, and remain open to other research directions given the opportunity. If you are looking for a prospective PhD student or would like to discuss potential supervision, please feel free to reach out at [thinhld@tlu.edu.vn](mailto:thinhld@tlu.edu.vn).
