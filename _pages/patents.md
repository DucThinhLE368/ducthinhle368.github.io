## Centrifugal Air Compressor Driven by an Electric Motor Using Conical Active Magnetic Bearings

**Abstract**

The invention relates to a centrifugal air compressor driven by an electric motor using conical active magnetic bearings, comprising an electric motor (30) arranged at the center and two centrifugal compressor units (10, 20) arranged symmetrically on either side, each unit having a housing (11, 21), an impeller (12, 22), and a rotating shaft (13, 23). The housing sections (11, 21, 31) are joined to form an airtight enclosure, and the rotating shafts (13, 23) are coaxially coupled to the motor rotor (33) via a flange joint (50). The two outer ends of the shaft assembly are supported and controlled by two conical active magnetic bearings (14, 24) whose inclined conical rotors (141, 241) face the center to generate a combined radial–axial electromagnetic force while balancing axial forces in two opposing directions. Position sensors (17, 18, 27, 28) detect the instantaneous deviation of the shaft assembly (13, 23) in the radial and axial directions. A magnetic bearing controller is configured to process multi-channel feedback signals and simultaneously control the operation of the conical active magnetic bearings (14, 24) to maintain high overall stiffness, reduce vibration, and ensure the dynamic stability of the shaft assembly during high-speed operation.

## Self-Balancing Electric Wheelchair System Integrated with Piezoelectric Sensors

**Abstract**

The invention relates to a multi-functional electric wheelchair integrating a lifting mechanism and therapeutic support system. The wheelchair comprises a frame (1) fitted with body-supporting components, including a headrest (3), a massage panel (4), and two footrests (8). The drive system is arranged with seven electric wheels (7), each containing an independent in-wheel motor (13); in addition, the wheels are driven by three electric motors (11) that provide torque when the wheelchair turns and climbs or descends steps. To enable configuration changes, seating-posture adjustment, and shock absorption, the frame (1) is connected to a linear actuation system comprising five main lifting cylinders (6), two footrest-adjustment cylinders (9), and springs (12). Notably, the wheelchair also integrates four piezoelectric elements (5) to support force sensing. The wheelchair further includes a battery mounting plate (10) to house the power supply for the entire motor and cylinder system, together with a speed control handle assembly (2) that allows the user to safely maneuver, accelerate, decelerate, and stop. This solution enables the wheelchair not only to move stably but also to flexibly change posture, providing relaxation features and enhancing user comfort and safety.
