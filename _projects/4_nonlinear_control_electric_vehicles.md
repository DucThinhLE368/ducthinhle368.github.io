**Application of nonlinear controllers and observers in motion control of electric vehicles**

- **Duration:** Nov. 2022 – Apr. 2024
- **Funding source:** Hanoi University of Science and Technology
- **Project number:** T2022-PC-003
- **Role:** Research Team Member

This project applies nonlinear and high-gain observer-based controllers to electric vehicle motion control, including traction and wheel-slip tracking under disturbances.
