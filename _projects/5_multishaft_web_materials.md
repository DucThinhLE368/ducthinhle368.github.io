**Control design for multi-shaft with web materials linkage systems**

- **Duration:** Jun. 2021 – Sep. 2022
- **Funding source:** Hanoi University of Science and Technology
- **Project number:** T2021-PC-001
- **Role:** Research Team Member

This project develops adaptive and fractional-order sliding-mode control strategies for multi-shaft drive systems, addressing matched and mismatched disturbances across linked shafts. Beyond roll-to-roll (web handling) systems, the same control framework extends to automotive applications such as traction and wheel-slip control.
