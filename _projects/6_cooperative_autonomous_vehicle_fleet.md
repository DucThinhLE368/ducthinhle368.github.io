**Cooperative control of an autonomous vehicle fleet integrated with wireless communication networks using optimization algorithms**

- **Current status:** Under review
- **Review progress:** The proposal passed the first round of peer review; the revised response report was submitted on 7 October 2026

## Project summary

This project investigates cooperative formation-control solutions for heterogeneous autonomous vehicles integrated with wireless sensor networks, addressing monitoring, transportation, search-and-rescue, and exploration missions across diverse environments. Motivated by the limitations of existing studies in multi-objective optimization and adaptability to real-world terrain, the project aims to develop and apply optimization algorithms and intelligent control methods that improve monitoring efficiency, autonomy, and stable network connectivity across the vehicle fleet.

The main research tasks include developing metaheuristic algorithms for coverage optimization and network self-deployment in unknown environments; planning trajectories and designing advanced tracking controllers using methods such as reinforcement learning and realistic vehicle-dynamics models; and developing formation-consensus controllers with collision-avoidance mechanisms under communication constraints. Through an approach spanning mathematical modeling and verification using high-fidelity simulation tools, the project seeks to establish a unified system that is robust against environmental disturbances. The expected results will support broader adoption of Internet of Things technologies and autonomous vehicle fleets in intelligent transportation, logistics, environmental exploration, and search-and-rescue applications.

**Keywords:** autonomous vehicles; cooperative control; wireless communication networks; wireless sensor networks; metaheuristic optimization algorithms; optimal control.
