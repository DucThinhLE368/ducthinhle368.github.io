Professional Summary

Lecturer at Thuyloi University, teaching in the Control Engineering and Automation and Robot Engineering and Intelligent Control programs, and researcher working on nonlinear and fault-tolerant control for electromechanical systems, electric vehicles, and renewable energy applications.
