Control of Non-Contact Drive Systems

HUST research project on fractional-order and disturbance-observer-based control for conical active magnetic bearings and non-contact drive systems

Research on Control of Non-Contact Drive Systems

  • Duration: Oct. 2023 – Sep. 2025
  • Funding source: Hanoi University of Science and Technology
  • Project number: T2023-PC-029
  • Role: Research Team Member

This project studies fractional-order and disturbance-observer-based control strategies for non-contact drive systems, including axial gap bearingless motors and conical active magnetic bearings, under time delays and supply faults.

  1. C17
    A Novel Conical Active Magnetic Bearing with a Three-pole-pair Structure
    Minh Huong Vu, Tien Manh Nguyen, Danh Giang Nguyen, and 3 more authors
    In 2025 Asia Meeting on Environment and Electrical Engineering (EEE-AM), 2025
    Co-author.
    Abs

    Currently, magnetic bearings are a major focus of research due to their ability to levitate and control the position of rotors, especially in the case of conical magnetic bearings. To mitigate the magnetic circuit saturation commonly found in traditional conical magnetic bearings, a new model and decoupling control structure have been developed, taking into account the influence of rotor imbalance forces during rotation. To control the system, an extended state observer and a sliding mode control are designed. In this paper, the results demonstrate the superior hardware performance and the improved ability of the new conical magnetic bearing to stabilize rotor displacement compared to the traditional design. To validate these findings, MATLAB simulation tools are employed to visualize and evaluate the mechanism and effectiveness of the proposed method.
